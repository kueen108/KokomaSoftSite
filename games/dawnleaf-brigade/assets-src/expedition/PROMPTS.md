# Expedition art

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-09-11. Original PNGs are retained here; WebP copies are bundled in public/assets/images.

Characters: white owl fire mage with burgundy hood and crystal staff, deer healer with green vestments and bell staff, blue wolf lancer with spear and round shield; charcoal imp bomber, turquoise ice wraith with icy wand, rust-armored boar sentinel with tower shield and flail. Painterly outlined animal-fantasy style matching the existing party atlas. Six equal rows; six walking frames per character and four frames per attack, hurt and guard state. Full bodies, separated cells, no labels.

The first outputs painted checkerboards instead of providing alpha. A second built-in edit preserved all poses and replaced the background with uniform #ff00ff for the game's chroma-key texture loader. No hand-painted character changes were made during bundling.

Background prompts: original painterly 16:9 side-scrolling battle lane at 65% height, lower third clear terrain, no characters or interface. Frost: snowy mountain pass and frozen bell tower under cyan aurora. Ember: volcanic forge valley, ruined foundry and distant lava behind basalt lane. Astral: ruined celestial palace, violet stars and ivory columns above clouds at predawn.
